Features

Everything for your next riff

Simple to start, with plenty of depth when you want it.

edit_note

Write tabs your way

Edit tabs with a touch-friendly fretboard, piano, or chord picker, or use keyboard shortcuts on PC.

play_circle

Hear what you write

Listen in real time, with hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, bends, vibrato, and palm mutes.

timer

Built-in Metronome

Practice with tap tempo, custom time signatures, per-beat accents, and selectable click sounds.

queue_music

Multi-track Songs

Combine up to three guitar tracks and one bass track in a single song.

volume_up

Mix your song

Adjust the playback tempo and balance the volume of every track.

tune

Transpose & retune

Change keys with a tap and switch tunings to match your instrument.

share

Export & Share

Share tabs as text, export and import .tabba files, or go Pro to export audio, sheet music and images.

security

Your Data Stays Yours

No accounts or analytics. Your tabs stay on your device, and you can back up your library anytime.
