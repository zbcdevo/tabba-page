Features

edit_note

Write tabs your way

Edit tabs with a touch-friendly fretboard, piano, or chord picker, or use keyboard shortcuts on PC.

play_circle

Hear what you write

Listen to your tab in real time with support for hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, bends, vibrato, and palm mutes.

timer

Built-in Metronome

Practice with tap tempo, custom time signatures, per-beat accents, and selectable click sounds.

queue_music

Multi-track Songs

Combine up to three guitar tracks and one bass track in a single song.

security

Your Data Stays Yours

No accounts or analytics. Your tabs stay on your device, and you can back up your library anytime.

share

Export & Share

Share tabs as text, export and import .tabba files, or render audio to .wav.
