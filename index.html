tabba

A calm, fast guitar & bass tab editor with a built-in player and Super Metronome. Works fully offline, and nothing is ever sent to the internet.

Report an issue / suggestion Contact Privacy Policy

Features

✍️

Write tabs your way

A touch-friendly Edit mode with fretboard, piano, and chord input, plus a fast keyboard-only PC mode for quick entry.

▶️

Hear what you write

A built-in player reads back your tab in real time, with hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, bends, vibrato, and palm mutes.

🥁

Super Metronome

Tap tempo, custom time signatures, per-beat accents, and a choice of click sounds to practice in time.

🎸

Multi-track songs

Layer up to 3 guitar tracks and 1 bass track in a single song, all playing together in sync.

🔒

Your data stays yours

No account, no backend, no analytics. Every tab lives on your device. Back up and restore your library any time.

📤

Export & share

Share tabs as plain text, or export/import the full-fidelity .tabba format, or render audio to .wav.

About

tabba is built by Zabba Labs, a small, independent effort to make a tab editor that's calm, fast, and respects your privacy. No ads, no tracking, no subscriptions holding your own tabs hostage.

The free tier covers up to 3 saved tabs with the full editor, player, and metronome. tabba Pro unlocks unlimited saved tabs, multi-track songs, audio export to .wav, and full .tabba import/export.
