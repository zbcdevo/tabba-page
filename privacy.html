Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Tabba ("the app") is developed by Zabba Labs. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is handled.

Summary

Tabba collects no data. It has no backend server, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no user accounts. The app works fully offline.

Data Storage

All tabs, settings, and preferences created in Tabba are stored only on your device in local storage. This data:

  • Is never transmitted over the internet
  • Is never sent to Zabba Labs or third parties
  • Is never used for tracking or advertising
  • Can be deleted anytime by clearing app data or uninstalling

Exported Files

Tabba lets you export tabs as plain text, .tabba files, or full library backups. These files save directly to your device or system share sheet. Tabba does not upload exported files anywhere.

Permissions

Tabba does not request permissions for contacts, location, camera, or microphone. Audio playback uses the Web Audio API on your device.

In-app Purchases

If you unlock Tabba Pro through Google Play Billing, Google processes the transaction. Tabba never sees or stores your payment details.

Children's Privacy

Tabba does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to Policy

Any updates to this policy will be posted here with a new date.
