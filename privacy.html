Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

tabba ("the app") is developed by Zabba Labs. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's handled.

Summary

tabba collects nothing. It has no backend server, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no user accounts. The app works fully offline.

Data storage

All tabs, settings, and preferences you create in tabba are stored only on your device, using your browser's/device's local storage. This data:

  • Is never transmitted over the internet
  • Is never sent to Zabba Labs or any third party
  • Is never used for advertising or tracking
  • Can be deleted at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it

Data you export

tabba lets you manually export your tabs as plain text or as .tabba files, or back up your entire library (.tabba backup file). These files are saved to your device or shared via your device's native share sheet. tabba does not upload them anywhere. What you do with an exported file (like sharing it yourself) is outside the app's control.

Permissions

tabba does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other sensitive device permission. Audio playback uses the Web Audio API, which runs entirely on-device.

In-app purchases (tabba Pro)

If you unlock tabba Pro via Google Play Billing, Google handles the transaction. tabba never sees or stores your payment details. The unlock state is stored locally on your device.

Children's privacy

tabba does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
